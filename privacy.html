Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Araaf Tractor Khata ("the app") handles your information.

Information we access

  • Google account basics: your name and email address, used only for signing in.
  • Google Drive: the app stores and syncs its own data file in your Google Drive. It accesses only the data created by the app, not your other files.
  • App data: the records you enter (customers, jobs, payments) are stored on your device and in your Google Drive.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and to back up and sync your records between your devices. We do not run our own servers to store your data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party. Your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive.

Deleting your data

You can delete the app's data from your Google Drive at any time and uninstall the app. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
