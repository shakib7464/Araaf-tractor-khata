Araaf Tractor Khata

Araaf Tractor Khata is an Android app for keeping tractor work and payment records (khata) in one place. You sign in with Google, and your data is backed up and synced through your own Google Drive.

  • Track jobs, customers and payments
  • Secure PIN / biometric lock
  • Sync across devices using Google Drive
